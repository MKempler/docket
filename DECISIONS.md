## Corrections adopted from review (Muse, 2026-11-05)

- The "1/40 vs 40/40" figure is Staffbox's, not ours. **Never** use it publicly unless reproduced by `evals/` on our own price book.
- One currency and tax rule for the demo; every number in README, tests, and video is generated by the engine and asserted by tests, never hand-typed. Default persona: **US plumber, USD** (pending user confirmation).
- Judge path is fully local (quote → local accept page → booked job). Online accept server + SMTP are optional extras.
- Stock and job-cost modules stay after the core loop and out of the video (user: scope is not a constraint).
